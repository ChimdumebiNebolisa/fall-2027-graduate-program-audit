Methodology
Decision rules and evidence limitations for interpreting the audit.
TopicMethod
Geographic scopeUnited States; Canada; EU-27 including Ireland; United Kingdom; Norway; Switzerland; Iceland.
Structured sourcesIPEDS; IRCC DLI; CICIC; ROR v2.10; national higher-education authorities; official university catalogues/pages.
Scholarly discoveryOpenAlex searches covering 2022 through the evidence date; discovery signals were rechecked on official appointment/program pages.
Screening stagesRegistry universe → mechanical field/program screen → research-fit discovery → program/faculty/funding/eligibility verification → hard gates → scoring → independent recheck.
Scoring rubricProfessor fit 30; department depth 15; funding 25; eligibility 15; degree/admissions alignment 10; application economics 5.
Hard gatesRecognized active institution, relevant research program, international eligibility, bachelor's entry or research master's route, verified supervisor, credible funding/full scholarship, compatible degree.
Recruiting statusConfirmed only with a current explicit statement or opening. Active lab/recent paper is not recruiting proof; silence remains unknown.
Funding definitionAssistantships existing is insufficient. Normal funding requires credible tuition and stipend/salary evidence; international-rate gaps remain explicit.
Admissions plausibilityQualitative categories only; no admission probabilities, ranking inference, or anecdotal-profile comparisons.
DeadlinesFall 2027 dates are used only when published. Older/current standing dates are labeled by their actual cycle/status.
Known gapsEurope ROR API fallback captured 81.31% of the reported filtered set; EHESO required authorization; blocked pages and unresolved Canadian crosswalks are manifested.
PrivacyCalendar/Gmail checks were bounded and read-only. No Calendar event changed, no Gmail draft created, and no email sent.
