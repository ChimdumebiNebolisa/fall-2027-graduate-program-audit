# Project Working Agreement

- Treat `config/*.yaml` and `src/graduate_audit/schema.py` as the shared contract.
- Regional workers may write only inside their assigned `data/processed/regions/<region>/` and corresponding regional evidence paths.
- The primary agent owns normalization, deduplication, scoring, reports, final outputs, and Git commits.
- Do not change scoring weights or enums in regional work.
- Every institution must receive a status; every exclusion must receive a reason and source.
- Use official sources for final program, admissions, funding, language, and faculty claims.
- Do not infer recruiting from publications, grants, current students, or an active lab.
- Preserve uncertainty when an official answer cannot be verified.
- Do not use Gmail or Calendar mutation tools.
